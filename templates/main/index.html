Global Impact

Our Commitment to the SDGs

We align our local conservation efforts with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals to ensure a lasting impact on our planet and its people.

Goal 13

Climate Action

We implement reforestation and greening projects to combat global warming and build community resilience against climate-related disasters.

Goal 15

Life on Land

Our commitment involves restoring degraded forests, protecting biodiversity, and promoting the sustainable management of Kenya's terrestrial ecosystems.

Goal 6

Clean Water & Sanitation

By conserving watersheds and promoting sustainable land-use practices, we ensure the protection and restoration of vital water-related ecosystems.

Goal 11

Sustainable Cities

We advocate for green urban spaces and community-led environmental initiatives to make human settlements inclusive, safe, and resilient.
