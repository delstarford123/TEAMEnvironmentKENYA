On The Ground

Expeditions & Environmental Activities

TEAMEnvironment KENYA engages in activities in all counties across the country and the region—in rural, urban, peri-urban areas, including residential and commercial environments.

Core Conservation Activities

  • Tree planting for sensitization and mobilization on reforestation, afforestation and agro-forestry/woodlots.
  • Town-cleaning.
  • River(s) adoption and rehabilitation.
  • Wetland reclamation.
  • Restoration of our water towers.

Eco-Tourism & Expeditions

  • Fundraising for conservation through nature walks, cycling, hikes, forest ‘bush whacks’, and media documentaries.
  • Game drives, zip-lines, board walks, canopy walks.
  • Tree platforms, bird watching bird hides, guided boat tours.

These activities and many others shall be undertaken within Game reserves/parks, forest/nature reserves, marine reserves/parks, wetlands, heritage sites, as well as biosphere reserves.
