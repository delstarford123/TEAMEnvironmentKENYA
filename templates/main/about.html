Who We Are

Socio-economic, Environmental & Humanitarian Membership Association

TEAMEnvironment KENYA is a Socio-economic, Environmental and Humanitarian Membership Association focused on Greening and Environmental Conservation.

Our Association is committed to addressing the sustainable development goals (SDGs) and protecting Kenya's landscapes from the impacts of climate change through community-based initiatives.

Become a Member

Vision

To be the leading champion in environmental conservation and humanitarian services, creating a greener and more sustainable future for all Kenyans.

Mission

To foster climate change adaptation and mitigation by catalyzing community-level interventions that enhance well-being, livelihood security, and environmental conservation.

Philosophy

“Build a Heritage for Future Generations of a Cleaner, Greener (Food & Water) Secure and a Peaceful Environment.”
