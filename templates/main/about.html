Who We Are

A Socio-economic, Environmental & Humanitarian Membership Association

Teamenvironment.KE.Africa is focused on Greening and Environmental Conservation through inclusive, community-based action. We believe that sustainable development starts with local commitment.

Our Association is dedicated to addressing the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and protecting Kenya's diverse landscapes from the impacts of climate change.

Join Our Community

Our Vision

To be the leading champion in environmental conservation and humanitarian services, creating a greener and more sustainable future for all Kenyans.

Our Mission

To foster climate change adaptation and mitigation by catalyzing community-level interventions that enhance well-being, livelihood security, and environmental conservation.

Our Philosophy

“Build a Heritage for Future Generations of a Cleaner, Greener (Food & Water) Secure and a Peaceful Environment.”

Our History

The Reforestation Journey

From humble roots in 2021 to a nationwide green network, see how our Green Army is transforming landscapes year by year.

2021

Founding & Humble Roots

Teamenvironment.KE.Africa is founded with a handful of visionary conservationists and establishes its first community seed propagation nurseries in Nairobi.

2022

KFS Strategic Partnership

TEK signs a formal Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the Kenya Forest Service (KFS), adopting key degraded acreage within the Ngong Hills ecosystem for active rehabilitation.

2023

50,000 Seedlings Milestone

The Green Army mobilizes local communities, successfully propagating and planting over 50,000 indigenous trees while establishing two community agroforestry hubs.

2024

Greening Digital Platform

Launch of our modern, offline-ready progressive web app (PWA) allowing volunteers, members, and supporters to easily track and book reforestation outings.

2025

150,000+ Trees & Automation

Crossing the 150,000 trees mark. Integrated automated payment gateways (M-Pesa, PayPal, PesaPal) providing immediate Certificates of Impact and receipts to digital supporters globally.

2026

Live Stewardship Hub

Unveiling the dynamic Stewardship Wall of Fame, progress milestones, and custom footprint certificate previews to maximize transparency and community engagement.
