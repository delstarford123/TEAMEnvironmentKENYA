About Us

Protecting, Conserving, and Restoring Kenya's Landscape.

Who We Are

We are committed to addressing sustainable development goals

At TEAMEnvironment KENYA, we are on a mission to protect, conserve, restore and rehabilitate landscapes, build climate resilience, and drive sustainable livelihoods for a greener future.

Our work aligns with Kenya’s National Climate Agenda, including the 15 Billion Tree Planting Initiative to Combat Climate Change and mitigate the effects of global warming towards the realization of Vision 2030.
