TEK Boilerplate Statement

"TEAMEnvironment KENYA is a Socio-economic, Environmental and Humanitarian Membership Association dedicated to Greening, climate resilience, and environmental conservation in Kenya. Guided by the philosophy of building a heritage of a cleaner, greener, food and water secure, and peaceful environment, the organization works in direct partnership with local communities and institutional actors like the Kenya Forest Service (KFS) to reforest water catchments and audit survival rates."
