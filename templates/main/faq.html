Frequently Asked Questions

Home / FAQ

How can I donate to TEK?

You can donate easily via M-Pesa using our floating "Donate" button or the link in the footer. Simply enter your phone number and the amount you wish to contribute, and you'll receive a prompt on your phone.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

TEAMEnvironment KENYA is a registered NGO in Kenya. We provide official receipts for all donations which can be used for tax purposes in accordance with Kenyan law.

How do I become a volunteer?

We're always looking for passionate individuals to join our cause. You can register via our Join Our Army page, and a member of our team will reach out to you.

Where does my money go?

Transparency is our priority. Donations go directly towards reforestation, community education programs, and the maintenance of our propagation centers. Track our progress on the Impact page.

Can organizations partner with TEK?

Yes, we welcome corporate partnerships and collaborations. Please visit our Contact Us page for partnership inquiries.
