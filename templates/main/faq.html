Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TEAMEnvironment KENYA and how you can get involved.

How can I donate to TEK?

You can donate easily via M-Pesa using our floating "Donate" button or the link in the footer. Simply enter your phone number and the amount you wish to contribute, and you'll receive a prompt on your phone.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

TEAMEnvironment KENYA is a registered NGO in Kenya. We provide receipts for all donations which can be used for tax purposes in accordance with Kenyan law.

How do I become a volunteer?

We're always looking for passionate individuals to join our cause. You can register via our Join Us page, and a member of our team will reach out to you with upcoming opportunities.

Where does my money go?

Transparency is key to our operations. Donations go directly towards our tree planting initiatives, community education programs, and the maintenance of restored sites. You can see our real-time impact on the Impact page.

Can organizations partner with TEK?

Yes, we welcome corporate partnerships and collaborations with other NGOs. Please contact us through our Contact Us page for partnership inquiries.
