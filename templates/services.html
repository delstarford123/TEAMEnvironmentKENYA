Our Services

Quality Environmental Solutions

Tree Planting (Growing)

We offer comprehensive tree growing services including Site and Species matching, ground preparation, and ongoing support to ensure the health and growth of your trees.

Climate Change Consultancy

Expert consultancy on climate change adaptation and mitigation. We help organizations understand environmental degradation and take action to protect our planet.

Supply of Quality Seedlings

We provide high-quality diverse tree seedlings sourced from KEFRI. Our 10-Million Tree Seedlings Propagation Center supports farmers and landowners.

Landscaping Services

Professional garden design and installation, lawn care, tree and shrub planting, hardscaping, irrigation system installation, and seasonal cleanup.

Environmental Impact Assessment

We assess potential environmental effects of proposed developments, ensuring projects comply with regulations and adhere to responsible sustainable practices.

Green Facility Initiative

Mobilizing the Green Facility initiative by partnering on PPP frameworks to enhance sustainable utility management of physical infrastructure and water catchments.
